## Usage Notes

- Replace every placeholder enclosed in brackets, such as **[Company Name]**, **[Position Title]**, and **[Hiring Manager's Name]**.
- Choose the referral and cover-letter version that best matches the vacancy.
- Keep only skills and responsibilities that accurately reflect your experience.
- Add one or two sentences explaining why you are interested in the specific company.
- When possible, include measurable results relevant to the position, such as the **15 percent reduction in delivery-driver waiting times**.
- Use **IT Support / Desktop Support** for end-user and help-desk vacancies.
- Use **Network / Field Support Technician** for installation, connectivity, cabling, and infrastructure vacancies.
- Use **Application Support / Technical Support Engineer** for production applications, logs, integrations, SQL, dashboards, and technical escalation roles.
